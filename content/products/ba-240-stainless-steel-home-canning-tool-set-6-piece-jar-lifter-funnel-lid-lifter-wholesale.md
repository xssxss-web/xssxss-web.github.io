The funnel's narrow spout fits inside the jar neck while the wide top accommodates ladles and scoops. The stainless steel construction conducts heat efficiently and is easy to clean between jars.

  The lid lifter is a magnetic wand that lifts canning lids from hot water, where they have been simmered to soften the sealing compound.   The bubble remover, also called a bubble popper or headspace tool, is a flat, non-metallic spatula used to release trapped air bubbles from filled jars before sealing. The tool also features measurement markings for checking headspace, the space between the food surface and the lid, which must be precise for proper sealing.

  The jar wrench grips jar lids for removal of sealed jars, providing leverage that makes opening vacuum-sealed jars effortless. The wrench accommodates both regular and wide-mouth jar lids. The tongs provide general-purpose handling of hot items during the canning process, including lifting utensils from boiling water sanitization and handling small jars that are too small for the jar lifter.

The tools are suitable for water bath canning, pressure canning, and general food preservation including jam, jelly, pickle, sauce, and relish making.
