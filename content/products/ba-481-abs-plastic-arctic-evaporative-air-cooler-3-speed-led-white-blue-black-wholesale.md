    answer: "Evaporative cooling uses the natural process of water evaporation to cool air. The cooler draws warm room air through a water-saturated cooling media or filter pad. This process is most effective in dry climates where relative humidity is below 60 percent. In humid climates, the cooling effect is reduced because the air is already saturated with moisture. No chemical refrigerants are used, making it environmentally friendly."
