Our BA-447 stainless steel milk frothing pitcher is a professional barista tool designed for steaming and frothing milk for espresso-based beverages including cappuccinos, lattes, flat whites, and macchiatos. The precision design and construction enable baristas to create microfoam milk texture and pour latte art with consistency and control.

The precision pour spout is the defining functional feature of this frothing pitcher. The narrow, V-shaped or pointed spout design allows the barista to direct the flow of milk precisely onto the coffee surface, enabling the creation of latte art patterns including hearts, rosettas, tulips, and swans. The spout shape controls the width and direction of the milk stream, which is essential for producing defined art patterns. Wider spout designs are available for simpler pour styles.

The 18/8 stainless steel construction provides excellent thermal conductivity, which is critical for milk frothing. The metal quickly conducts heat from the steam wand to the milk, allowing the barista to monitor milk temperature by holding the pitcher. The pitcher should be cold to the touch when milk is first added and become warm as the milk reaches the target temperature of approximately 60-65 degrees Celsius. Stainless steel is also rust-resistant, food-safe, and will not impart flavors to the milk.

The multiple capacity options accommodate different beverage volumes and service requirements. The 350ml pitcher is ideal for single-cappuccino service, the 600ml pitcher is the standard barista size for most latte and cappuccino orders, and the 1000ml pitcher is suited for large beverages or multiple drink preparation. The pitcher should be filled to approximately one-third capacity before frothing to allow room for milk expansion during aeration.

The matte black coated finish is a premium aesthetic option visible in the showroom display. The black PVD or powder coating provides a modern, sophisticated appearance that coordinates with contemporary espresso machine designs. The coating is applied over the stainless steel, so the thermal conductivity and food safety properties are maintained. The mirror-polished silver finish is the traditional option, providing a classic barista appearance that is easy to clean and maintain.
