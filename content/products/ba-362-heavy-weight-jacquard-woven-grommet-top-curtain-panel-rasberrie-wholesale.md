Our heavy weight jacquard woven grommet top curtain panel, branded RASBERRIE, is a premium window treatment crafted from a substantial jacquard woven fabric in dark charcoal and navy tones. The label reads 'HEAVY WEIGHT JACQUARD WINDOW CURTAIN', signalling a richly textured, luxurious panel that adds depth, weight, and a sense of occasion to formal living rooms, dining rooms, and master bedrooms.

Jacquard weaving produces an integrated pattern with a tactile, raised texture that cannot be replicated by printing. The heavy weight construction gives the panel excellent drape and a substantial feel that hangs beautifully and resists fluttering in drafts. The dense weave also provides a degree of light reduction and insulation, helping to moderate room temperature and soften outside light for a cozy, controlled ambiance.

The dark charcoal and navy colorway is sophisticated and grounding, working well with metallic accents, rich woods, and jewel-tone accessories. This palette is favored for formal and transitional interiors where a dramatic, enveloping window treatment is desired. The jacquard texture catches light subtly, so the panel reveals different depths of color as the lighting changes through the day.

Each panel measures 140x260cm, a floor-length drop that suits tall windows and creates an elegant pooled or just-skimming finish. The grommet top header with metal rings supports the heavier fabric weight evenly and allows smooth opening and closing. Because the fabric is heavy, the grommets and header are reinforced to prevent stretching or tearing over time.

Quality control for heavy weight jacquard focuses on pattern consistency across the panel, seam integrity, grommet reinforcement, and dimensional stability. The substantial fabric must hang straight without skewing, and the jacquard pattern must align cleanly at the seams. Each panel is individually polybagged and packed to protect the textured surface and prevent compression creasing during transit.

This RASBERRIE jacquard panel targets the upper-mid and premium segment of the curtain market. It appeals to interior design stores, hotel suppliers, and home decor distributors serving customers who want a luxurious, weighted window treatment. The combination of heavy jacquard texture, rich dark color, and durable construction delivers a high-value product with strong retail presentation.
