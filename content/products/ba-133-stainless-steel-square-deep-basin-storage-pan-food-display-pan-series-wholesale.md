Manufactured from thickened 304 stainless steel ranging from 0.6mm to 1.0mm, these basins outperform lighter pans in abusive environments.

The series covers four practical sizes: 30x20x15cm for compact prep stations and ingredient washing; 35x25x18cm for medium-volume marinating and cold storage; 40x30x20cm for bulk food holding and display; and 45x35x22cm for maximum capacity soaking, ice bathing, and buffet cold well integration. The generous depth-to-footprint ratio maximizes usable volume while minimizing counter space consumption.

Unlike standard sheet-metal pans that can flex or dent under the weight of ice, water, or dense food loads, these deep basins maintain their rectangular geometry through reinforced corners and thicker wall construction. The rolled top edge provides a comfortable carry surface for gloved hands and prevents the sharp cut hazards common with raw stamped metal edges.

Square geometry offers distinct advantages over round bowls in commercial settings: efficient packing in refrigerated storage racks, stable alignment on rectangular prep tables, and maximum space utilization in buffet cold wells and ice displays. The flat sides also facilitate ingredient agitation and allow stacking with minimal shifting.

Primary applications include soaking dried goods, washing produce, marinating proteins in bulk brines, holding ice baths for shellfish display, and presenting cold buffet items in self-service environments. The deep walls contain splashes during washing and prevent cross-contamination between adjacent food items on crowded prep lines.

The brushed stainless steel exterior presents a professional, understated appearance suitable for open kitchen designs and front-of-house display stations. The interior is smooth-finished for easy soil release and efficient cleaning. All materials comply with FDA, LFGB, and EU food contact regulations, with full traceability to certified steel mills.
