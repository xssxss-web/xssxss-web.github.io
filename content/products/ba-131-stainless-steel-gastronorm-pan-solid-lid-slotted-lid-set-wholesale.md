Complete your gastronorm pan system with our precision-engineered stainless steel lids, designed to pair perfectly with BA-130 gastronorm pans and all standard GN-size containers. Available in two functional variants: solid lids for maximum sealing and freshness retention, and slotted lids that accommodate serving utensils while maintaining food coverage and temperature control.

Each lid is stamped from 304 food-grade stainless steel with the same quality standards as our pans. The solid lid features a flat, tight-fitting design with a peripheral rolled edge that nests securely into the pan rim, creating an effective barrier against air exposure, moisture loss, and cross-contamination. This makes it ideal for overnight refrigeration, food transport, and prep-ahead workflows in busy commercial kitchens.

The slotted lid variant incorporates precision-cut slots or notches positioned to allow ladles, serving spoons, tongs, and portion scoops to remain inserted in the food while the lid stays in place. This design is essential for buffet lines where guests serve themselves: it minimizes heat loss, reduces evaporation, prevents splatter, and maintains hygiene by keeping utensils from contacting surfaces.

Both lid styles match the full GN size matrix: 1/1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, and 1/6. The stacking geometry is optimized so that lidded pans can be safely stacked without shifting during storage or transport. The lightweight yet rigid construction (150-600g depending on size) makes handling effortless for kitchen staff while providing the durability expected in professional environments.

The brushed stainless steel finish resists fingerprints and water spots, maintaining a clean, professional appearance on open buffet lines. All edges are deburred and smoothly rolled to eliminate sharp points that could injure staff or damage gloves. The material is non-reactive and will not impart metallic flavors to acidic or alkaline foods.

These lids are indispensable accessories for hotels, resorts, convention centers, cruise ships, airline catering facilities, and institutional food service operations. When paired with BA-130 pans, they create a complete serving and storage system. Custom slot configurations, logo embossing, and colored silicone rim inserts are available through our OEM program.
