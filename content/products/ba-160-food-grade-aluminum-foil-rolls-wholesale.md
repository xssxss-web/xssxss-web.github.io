Our food-grade aluminum foil roll series is manufactured from premium 8011 aluminum alloy with thickness ranging from 0.01mm to 0.025mm, ensuring excellent strength and flexibility for both household and commercial applications. Each roll features a serrated cutting edge for effortless tearing, eliminating the need for scissors or additional cutting tools during busy kitchen operations.

The household line offers a convenient 25 square feet roll (approximately 30cm x 7.6m), perfect for daily home cooking, baking, and food storage needs. The compact size fits easily in standard kitchen drawers while providing enough coverage for dozens of meals. For commercial users, we offer bulk rolls in 30m, 50m, and 100m lengths at 30cm width, designed for high-volume restaurants, bakeries, catering services, and food processing facilities.

Our HEAVY DUTY reinforced option features increased thickness of 0.02mm to 0.025mm, providing superior puncture resistance and durability. This makes it ideal for wrapping foods with bones, sharp edges, or heavy sauces that would tear standard foil. The heavy-duty grade maintains structural integrity even when folded multiple times.

The aluminum foil is directly food-contact safe and withstands temperatures up to 220°C, making it suitable for oven baking, grill covering, and freezer storage. Its excellent oxygen and moisture barrier properties help maintain food freshness, prevent flavor transfer between items, and reduce food waste by extending shelf life in refrigerated and frozen conditions.

Beyond food applications, the foil serves as an effective heat reflector for baking ovens, a protective cover for stovetops during messy cooking, a polishing cloth for stainless steel surfaces, and a temporary funnel or piping bag for decorating. The versatility of this product makes it an essential item in every professional and home kitchen.

All rolls are individually packed in color-printed retail boxes with shrink film protection, ensuring the foil remains clean and undamaged during storage and transportation. The attractive packaging design supports direct retail display in supermarkets, kitchenware stores, and online marketplaces, reducing the need for additional repackaging.
