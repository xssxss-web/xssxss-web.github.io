Yiwu is suitable for small commodity wholesale, especially when buyers need many SKUs, flexible MOQ and fast sample comparison. For home and kitchen products, the key is to avoid random sourcing and focus on related categories that can be combined into mixed orders.

A practical starting point is to choose 20-50 products across kitchen gadgets, storage products, cleaning tools and bathroom accessories, then test demand through inquiries before expanding the catalog.
