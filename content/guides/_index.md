## Wholesale Sourcing Guides

These guides are written for importers, distributors, Amazon sellers, and retail buyers who source home and kitchen products from China. Each guide covers a specific part of the wholesale sourcing process — from understanding MOQ and pricing, to managing shipping, customization, and quality control.

Whether you are placing your first trial order or optimizing an established supply chain, these guides will help you make better purchasing decisions and avoid common pitfalls.

## Why These Guides Matter

Sourcing home and kitchen products from China is not difficult, but it is full of small decisions that affect your margin, timeline, and product quality. Many buyers learn these lessons the hard way — through delayed shipments, quality disputes, or unexpected costs.

These guides distill years of real sourcing experience into practical, actionable information. We update them regularly based on market changes, new factory capabilities, and feedback from buyers.
