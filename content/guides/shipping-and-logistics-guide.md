## Choosing the Right Shipping Method

The right shipping method depends on three factors: **order volume**, **urgency**, and **budget**. Here is a quick reference:

| Method | Best For | Transit Time | Cost (per kg equivalent) |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------------------------|
| Express courier (DHL/FedEx/UPS) | Samples, urgent small orders | 3-7 days | $6-12/kg |
| Air freight | Urgent orders, 50-300 kg | 5-10 days | $4-8/kg |
| LCL sea freight | Small orders, 0.5-8 CBM | 25-40 days | $80-150/CBM |
| FCL sea freight | Large orders, 15+ CBM | 20-35 days | $1,200-3,500/container |

**Rule of thumb**: If your shipment is under 100 kg and you need it fast, use express. If it is under 8 cubic meters (CBM) and you can wait, use LCL. If it is over 15 CBM, use FCL.

## Sea Freight: The Most Common Choice

### FCL (Full Container Load)

FCL means you book an entire container for your shipment. This is the most cost-effective option for orders that fill at least half a container.

| Container Size | Capacity | Typical Products |
|---------------|----------|-----------------|
| 20ft (20'GP) | ~28 CBM, ~17 tons | 5,000-8,000 tumblers, or mixed SKUs |
| 40ft (40'GP) | ~58 CBM, ~20 tons | 12,000-18,000 mixed kitchen products |
| 40ft HQ (40'HC) | ~68 CBM, ~20 tons | Lightweight but voluminous products |

**Advantages of FCL:**
- Lowest per-unit shipping cost
- No risk of cross-contamination from other shippers' goods
- Faster customs clearance (sealed container)
- Fixed sailing schedule

### LCL (Less than Container Load)

LCL means your goods share a container with other shippers' cargo. This is ideal for smaller orders.

**How LCL works:**
1. Your goods are delivered to a consolidation warehouse in Yiwu or Ningbo
2. The freight forwarder packs your goods into a shared container
3. Goods are deconsolidated at the destination port
4. You pick up or arrange delivery from the destination warehouse

**LCL cost calculation:**

LCL charges based on the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight (1 CBM = 363 kg). Example:

- Shipment: 2 CBM, 400 kg
- Volumetric weight: 2 × 363 = 726 kg
- Chargeable weight: 726 kg (the greater value)
- Rate: $85/CBM
- Total LCL cost: 2 × $85 = $170 (plus destination charges)

**Watch out for destination charges**: LCL shipments have destination fees (THC, documentation, customs clearance) that can add $150-400 to the total. Always ask for a door-to-door or door-to-port quote that includes these.

## Air Freight

Air freight is faster but significantly more expensive. It is best for:

- **Urgent replenishment** of fast-selling SKUs
- **Seasonal products** that must arrive before a holiday deadline
- **High-value, low-weight items** (e.g., precision kitchen tools)

### How Air Freight Works

1. Goods are delivered to Shanghai Pudong (PVG) or Hangzhou (HGH) airport
2. Goods fly to your destination airport
3. You or your broker clear customs and pick up the cargo

**Transit time**: 5-10 days door-to-door (including customs on both sides)

**Cost factors:**
- Chargeable weight = greater of actual weight or volumetric weight (1 CBM = 167 kg for air)
- Rates vary by season (peak: September-December)
- Fuel surcharges apply

## Express Courier

For samples and small urgent orders, express courier is the simplest option.

| Courier | Best For | Typical Cost (China to US) |
|---------|----------|---------------------------|
| DHL | Fastest delivery, good tracking | $35-55 for 1 kg, $6-8/kg for 10+ kg |
| FedEx | Reliable to North America | $30-50 for 1 kg, $5-7/kg for 10+ kg |
| UPS | Good for European destinations | $35-55 for 1 kg, $6-8/kg for 10+ kg |

**Tips for express shipping:**
- Use our DHL/FedEx account for discounted rates (up to 50% off published rates)
- Declare accurate product value — under-declaring risks customs seizure
- For samples, use "Sample, no commercial value" to reduce duty in some countries

## Consolidation: The Yiwu Advantage

Consolidation is the process of combining goods from multiple factories into a single shipment. This is one of the biggest advantages of sourcing from Yiwu.

### How Consolidation Works

1. You place orders with 5 different factories for different products
2. Each factory ships goods to our Yiwu warehouse
3. We inspect each batch upon arrival
4. We repack and consolidate into one container or shipment
5. You receive one shipment with all products and one set of documents

### Consolidation Cost Savings

| Scenario | Without Consolidation | With Consolidation |
|----------|----------------------|-------------------|
| 5 factories, 5 LCL shipments | 5 × $250 = $1,250 | 1 × $400 = $400 |
| 5 separate customs clearances | 5 × $150 = $750 | 1 × $150 = $150 |
| 5 destination warehouse fees | 5 × $80 = $400 | 1 × $80 = $80 |
| **Total** | **$2,400** | **$630** |
| **Savings** | | **$1,770 (74%)** |

## Customs and Import Duties

| Document | Purpose | Who Provides |
|----------|---------|-------------|

| Packing List | Cargo details | We provide |

| Product test reports | Compliance verification | Factory or third-party lab (if needed) |

## DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Service

For buyers who want a hassle-free experience, we offer DDP service to selected destinations (US, EU, UK, Australia, Canada).

**What DDP includes:**
- Factory pickup
- Export customs clearance in China
- Ocean or air freight
- Import customs clearance at destination
- Duty and tax payment
- Final delivery to your warehouse

**DDP pricing**: Typically 15-25% more than FOB + freight + duty calculated separately, but eliminates all customs and logistics headaches.

## Tracking Your Shipment

Once your order ships, we provide:

1. **Container number or tracking number** — Track on shipping line website or courier website
2. **Bill of lading copy** — For your records and customs broker
3. **Estimated arrival date** — Updated weekly
4. **Port-to-port status updates** — Departure, transit, arrival, customs hold (if any)

## Common Shipping Problems and Solutions

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Customs hold | Missing or incorrect documents | Provide complete docs; work with experienced broker |
| Container delay | Port congestion, peak season | Book 2-3 weeks earlier during Sept-Dec |
| Damage in transit | Poor packing, rough handling | Use palletizing; add "Fragile" labels; insure shipment |
| Weight discrepancy | Factory over/under packs | Verify carton count and weight at consolidation |
| Unexpected destination charges | LCL destination fees not quoted upfront | Request door-to-door quotes; compare total cost |
