## Understanding MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity)

MOQ is the smallest quantity a factory will accept for a production run. In Yiwu, MOQs are generally lower than in other Chinese manufacturing hubs because the market is built for small commodity wholesale.

### What Determines MOQ

| Factor | Effect on MOQ | Example |
|--------|--------------|---------|
| Customization level | Higher customization = higher MOQ | Stock tumbler: 100 pcs. Custom logo: 500 pcs. Custom color: 1,000 pcs |
| Material availability | Standard materials = lower MOQ | 304 stainless steel (common): 200 pcs. Titanium coating (rare): 2,000 pcs |
| Production method | Automated = lower MOQ | Injection molding: 500 pcs. Hand-blown glass: 100 pcs |
| Packaging | Standard poly bag = lower MOQ | Custom color box: 1,000 pcs. Retail-ready blister pack: 2,000 pcs |
| Season | Off-season = lower MOQ | March-June (slow season): flexible. September-December (peak): strict |

### Mixed MOQ: The Yiwu Advantage

Many Yiwu factories allow "mixed MOQ" — combining multiple SKUs to reach the minimum. For example:

- Factory MOQ: 500 pcs
- You want: SKU A (200 pcs) + SKU B (150 pcs) + SKU C (150 pcs)
- Total: 500 pcs — order accepted

This is especially valuable for Amazon sellers and retail buyers who need product variety but cannot commit to 500 pcs of a single SKU.

## How Wholesale Pricing Works

### Price Tiers

Unit price drops as quantity increases. Here is a typical tier structure for a stainless steel tumbler:

| Quantity | Unit Price (FOB) | vs. Base Price |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| 100-299 pcs | $4.80 | Base price |
| 300-499 pcs | $4.20 | -12.5% |
| 500-999 pcs | $3.60 | -25% |
| 1,000-2,999 pcs | $3.20 | -33% |
| 3,000-5,000 pcs | $2.80 | -42% |
| 5,000+ pcs | $2.50 | -48% |

**The sweet spot** for most buyers is 500-1,000 pcs per SKU — you get most of the price benefit without overcommitting inventory.

### What's Included in FOB Price

FOB (Free On Board) price includes:

- Product manufacturing cost
- Inner packaging (poly bag or bubble wrap)
- Outer carton packing
- Export carton marking
- Factory-to-port trucking
- China customs export clearance

FOB price does **not** include:

- Ocean freight or air freight
- Insurance
- Destination port charges
- Import duties and taxes
- Customs broker fees
- Inland trucking at destination

### Hidden Costs to Budget For

| Cost Item | Typical Range | When It Applies |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| Sample cost | $3-8 per piece | Before bulk order |
| Sample shipping | $30-60 (DHL/FedEx) | Before bulk order |
| Custom logo setup | $30-80 one-time | First order with logo |
| Custom color box printing plate | $50-150 one-time | First order with custom packaging |
| QC inspection fee | $100-200 per order | Optional but recommended |
| Consolidation fee | $50-100 per shipment | When mixing multiple factories |
| Palletizing | $8-15 per pallet | Optional for LCL shipments |

## Payment Terms

### Standard Payment Structure

Most Yiwu suppliers use a two-payment structure:

1. **30% deposit** — Paid before production starts. Triggers factory to begin manufacturing.
2. **70% balance** — Paid before shipment, after quality inspection is passed and loading photos are provided.

### Payment Methods

| Method | Best For | Fees | Risk Level |
|--------|----------|------|------------|
| Bank transfer (T/T) | Most orders | $20-40 flat fee | Low (with proper contract) |
| PayPal | Samples, small orders under $500 | 4-4.5% + $0.30 | Very low (buyer protection) |
| Alibaba Trade Assurance | Orders via Alibaba | 2-3% of order value | Low (escrow protection) |
| Letter of Credit (L/C) | Large orders over $20,000 | $100-300 bank fees | Very low (bank guarantee) |

### Negotiating Payment Terms

For first-time orders, expect to pay 30% deposit upfront. As you build a relationship with repeat orders, you can negotiate:

- **20% deposit** instead of 30%
- **Payment after bill of lading copy** (balance against B/L copy, not before shipment)
- **Net 15-30 terms** for established customers (rare but possible after 3+ orders)

## Calculating Your Landed Cost

Landed cost is the total cost to get the product to your warehouse. Here is a formula:

```
Landed cost per unit = 
  (Unit FOB price) 
  + (Freight per unit) 
  + (Insurance per unit) 
  + (Duty per unit) 
  + (Customs broker per unit) 
  + (Inland trucking per unit)
```

### Example Calculation

**Product**: 40oz Stainless Steel Tumbler (BA-001)
**Order**: 500 pcs
**FOB price**: $3.60/pc
**Shipping**: LCL to Los Angeles, $450 total
**Insurance**: 0.3% of order value = $5.40
**US import duty**: 3.4% (HS code 9617000000 for vacuum flasks)
**Customs broker**: $150 flat
**Inland trucking** (port to warehouse): $200

| Cost Item | Per Unit |
|-----------|----------|
| FOB price | $3.60 |
| Freight ($450 / 500) | $0.90 |
| Insurance ($5.40 / 500) | $0.01 |
| Duty ($1,800 × 3.4% / 500) | $0.12 |
| Customs broker ($150 / 500) | $0.30 |
| Inland trucking ($200 / 500) | $0.40 |
| **Total landed cost** | **$5.33** |

At a retail price of $19.99, your gross margin would be approximately 73% — healthy for a direct-to-consumer or Amazon FBA model.

## Tips for Price Negotiation

1. **Don't negotiate on the first inquiry** — Build rapport first. Ask about factory capacity, lead times, and product details before discussing price.
2. **Use volume as leverage** — If you plan to reorder, mention your annual purchase projection. Factories discount for repeat business.
3. **Compare 3 suppliers** — Get quotes from at least 3 factories for the same product. This gives you a realistic price benchmark.
4. **Ask what's included** — "Is this price FOB Yiwu or FOB Ningbo? Does it include inner box? What about barcode labeling?" These details affect true cost.
5. **Don't push too hard** — Yiwu margins are already thin. If you push below a reasonable price, the factory will cut corners on material quality to compensate.
