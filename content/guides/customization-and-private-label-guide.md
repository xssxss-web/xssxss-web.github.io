## Levels of Customization

Customization ranges from simple logo addition to full product development. Understanding the levels helps you choose the right approach for your budget and timeline.

| Level | What's Involved | MOQ | Setup Cost | Lead Time |
|-------|----------------|-----|------------|-----------|
| Level 1: Logo printing | Add your logo to existing product | 200-500 pcs | $30-80 | +3-5 days |
| Level 2: Custom color | Match product to your Pantone color | 500-1,000 pcs | $50-150 | +7-10 days |
| Level 3: Custom packaging | Design your own box or insert | 500-1,000 pcs | $50-200 (plate) | +10-15 days |
| Level 4: Minor modification | Change handle shape, lid style, capacity | 1,000-3,000 pcs | $200-500 (tooling) | +15-25 days |
| Level 5: Full ODM | Design a new product from scratch | 3,000-5,000 pcs | $500-5,000 (mold) | +30-60 days |

Most buyers start at Level 1-2 and move up as their brand grows.

## Logo Printing Methods

### 1. Screen Printing

**Best for**: Flat surfaces, simple 1-4 color logos
**Products**: Tumblers, bottles, ceramic mugs, glassware
**Durability**: 2-3 years with normal use
**Cost**: $0.15-0.30 per piece
**Setup**: $30-50 (screen plate per color)
**MOQ**: 200 pcs

### 2. Laser Engraving

**Best for**: Metal surfaces, premium feel, permanent marking
**Products**: Stainless steel tumblers, flasks, knives, utensils
**Durability**: Permanent (etched into metal)
**Cost**: $0.20-0.40 per piece
**Setup**: $20-40 (no plate needed)
**MOQ**: 100 pcs

### 3. UV Digital Printing

**Best for**: Full-color logos, photographic images, gradient designs
**Products**: Tumblers, bottles, ceramic, glass, plastic
**Durability**: 1-2 years (UV-cured ink)
**Cost**: $0.25-0.50 per piece
**Setup**: $10-30 (digital, no plate)
**MOQ**: 100 pcs

### 4. Heat Transfer / Water Decal

**Best for**: Complex multi-color designs on curved surfaces
**Products**: Tumblers, mugs, bottles
**Durability**: 1-2 years
**Cost**: $0.20-0.45 per piece
**Setup**: $30-60 (transfer film or decal)
**MOQ**: 200 pcs

### 5. Embossing / Debossing

**Best for**: Leather, silicone, packaging
**Products**: Silicone kids dining sets, packaging boxes
**Durability**: Permanent
**Cost**: $0.10-0.25 per piece
**Setup**: $50-100 (mold)
**MOQ**: 500 pcs

## Custom Color Matching

### How Pantone Matching Works

1. You provide a Pantone (PMS) color code (e.g., Pantone 286 C for a specific blue)
2. The factory mixes powder coating or paint to match that code
3. A color sample is produced for your approval
4. Once approved, all production uses that exact formula

### Color Customization by Material

| Material | Method | MOQ | Color Accuracy |
|----------|--------|-----|----------------|
| Powder-coated stainless steel | Electrostatic spray | 500 pcs | ±5% (very close) |
| Anodized aluminum | Electrochemical | 1,000 pcs | ±3% (excellent) |
| Plastic injection | Masterbatch dye | 1,000 pcs | ±5% (very close) |
| Ceramic glaze | Kiln firing | 1,000 pcs | ±10% (slight variation) |
| Silicone | Pigment mix | 500 pcs | ±5% (very close) |
| Glass (colored) | Glass color batch | 3,000 pcs | ±10% (slight variation) |

**Important**: Always request a physical color sample before mass production. Digital photos do not accurately represent colors.

### Designing Your Custom Box

For a custom retail box, you will need to provide:

1. **Box dimensions** — We calculate based on product size
2. **Die-line template** — We provide the template; you place your design
3. **Print files** — AI, PDF, or EPS format, CMYK color mode, 300 DPI
4. **Finish specifications** — Matte/glossy lamination, spot UV, foil stamping, embossing

**Typical timeline for custom packaging**: 5-7 days for design proof, 7-10 days for production.

## Barcode and Labeling

### For Amazon FBA Sellers

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| FNSKU label | Applied to each unit or printed on packaging |
| Expiration dating | Required for some categories (not home & kitchen) |

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| UPC/EAN barcode | 12 or 13 digit barcode for scanning |
| Country of origin label | Required by most countries |

| Market | Certification | Products | Cost |
|--------|--------------|----------|------|

| USA | Proposition 65 | Products with potential chemical exposure | $150-400/test |
| EU | CE / LFGB | Food contact, general product safety | $200-600/test |
| EU | REACH | Chemical compliance for all products | $100-300/test |
| UK | UKCA | Post-Brexit UK safety mark | $200-500/test |
| Global | BPA-free test | Plastic and silicone products | $80-200/test |

**Important**: Many of our factories already have current test reports. Ask us about available certifications before paying for new tests.

## The Customization Process Step by Step

### Step 1: Define Your Requirements (Day 0)

- Logo design file (vector format: AI, EPS, or SVG)
- Target Pantone colors
- Packaging design concept
- Compliance requirements for your market
- Barcode numbers (if applicable)

### Step 2: Get a Customization Quote (Day 1-2)

We confirm with the factory:
- Feasibility of each customization request
- Setup costs and per-unit costs
- MOQ for each customization level
- Updated lead time

### Step 3: Approve Samples (Day 5-15)

The factory produces:
- A customized product sample with your logo and color
- A packaging sample (if custom packaging)
- A color match sample (if custom color)

You review and approve (or request changes).

### Step 4: Mass Production (Day 15-45)

- Production begins after sample approval and deposit payment
- In-line quality checks during production
- Logo placement and quality verified at inspection
- Packaging assembled and labeled per your specifications

### Step 5: Final Inspection and Shipment (Day 45-50)

- Final random inspection at AQL 2.5
- Verify logo alignment, color accuracy, packaging quality
- Barcode scan test
- Loading photos before container sealing

## Common Customization Mistakes

1. **Don't use low-resolution logos** — Vector files (AI/EPS) are required for crisp printing. JPG/PNG files produce blurry results.
2. **Don't assume colors match across materials** — Your Pantone blue will look different on powder-coated steel vs. anodized aluminum vs. printed cardboard. Always approve physical samples.
3. **Don't forget packaging dimensions** — A custom box that is 5mm too small will damage the product. We calculate exact dimensions for you.
4. **Don't skip barcode verification** — Test-scan your barcodes before mass production. A barcode that doesn't scan costs sales.
5. **Don't change specs mid-production** — Changes after production starts cause delays, waste materials, and increase costs. Lock in specifications at sample approval.
