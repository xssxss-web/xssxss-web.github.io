## Who We Are

OMLMO Home & Kitchen Wholesale is a focused B2B online showroom based in Yiwu, China — the world's largest small commodity wholesale market. Unlike general trading companies that list everything from electronics to toys, we specialize exclusively in home and kitchen products. This focus lets us go deeper into product categories, maintain stricter quality control, and build stronger factory relationships.

We serve importers, distributors, supermarket buyers, Amazon FBA sellers, Shopify store owners, and promotional product companies across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

## What We Do

We bridge the gap between Yiwu factories and global wholesale buyers. Our role covers:

- **Product curation** — We select products that have proven retail demand, reliable factory supply, and competitive pricing. Every product on our showroom has been physically inspected.
- **Supplier vetting** — We work directly with factories we have visited in person. No middleman layers, no blind sourcing.
- **Mixed order consolidation** — Buyers can combine products from different categories and different factories into a single shipment. This is the core advantage of sourcing from Yiwu.
- **Quality control** — Pre-shipment inspection on every order. We check materials, dimensions, surface finish, functionality, and packaging integrity before goods leave China.
- **Customization support** — Custom logo printing, Pantone color matching, custom packaging design, retail-ready barcodes, and minor product modifications.
- **Logistics coordination** — Sea freight, air freight, and express shipping with warehouse consolidation in Yiwu.

## Why Source from Yiwu

Yiwu International Trade City is the largest wholesale market of its kind in the world, with over 75,000 booths across 4 million square meters. For home and kitchen buyers, the advantages are clear:

1. **SKU density** — Thousands of suppliers within walking distance. You can compare 20 factories making the same product in a single afternoon.
2. **Low MOQ flexibility** — Yiwu factories are accustomed to small-batch wholesale. Many products start at 100-500 pcs MOQ, compared to 5,000+ pcs in other manufacturing hubs.
3. **Mixed-order consolidation** — Buy 10 SKUs from 5 different factories, and we consolidate everything into one container shipment. This dramatically reduces per-SKU shipping cost.
4. **Fast sample turnaround** — Samples are typically available within 3-7 days. We can collect samples from multiple suppliers and ship them together.
5. **Market responsiveness** — Yiwu suppliers move fast on trends. New drinkware designs, seasonal colors, and trending products appear in the market within weeks.

## Quality Assurance Process

Every order goes through a 4-step quality process:

1. **Pre-production sample approval** — Before mass production starts, we send a confirmed sample for your sign-off.
2. **In-line inspection** — During production, we check 10-15% of output for material consistency and workmanship.
3. **Final random inspection** — AQL 2.5 inspection on finished goods before packing. We check dimensions, surface quality, functionality, and packaging.
4. **Loading supervision** — We monitor container loading to ensure correct quantities and proper stacking.
