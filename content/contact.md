## Get in Touch

We respond to all inquiries within **24 hours** (Monday-Saturday, GMT+8). To get the fastest and most accurate quotation, please include the following in your message:

- **SKU numbers** — Browse our [product catalog](/products/) and copy the SKU (e.g., BA-001, KG-024)
- **Target quantity** — How many pieces per SKU
- **Destination country** — For shipping cost calculation
- **Customization requirements** — Logo, color, packaging, barcodes, etc.
- **Timeline** — When you need the goods to arrive

## Common Questions

**Can I order samples before placing a bulk order?**

Yes. Samples are available for most products at $3-8 per piece plus shipping. For confirmed orders over $5,000, sample costs are credited back.

**What is the minimum order quantity?**

Standard MOQ is 500 pcs per SKU, but many products support 100-300 pcs for trial orders. Mixed-SKU orders are welcome — you can combine different products to reach container volume.

**Do you support custom branding?**

Yes. We offer logo printing (screen print, laser engrave, UV print), custom Pantone colors, custom packaging design, retail-ready barcodes, and full ODM product development.

**What shipping methods do you offer?**

Sea freight (FCL and LCL), air freight, and express courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS). We also offer door-to-door DDP service for selected destinations. Goods are consolidated at our Yiwu warehouse before shipment.

**Can I visit your office or factories?**

Yes, visits are welcome by appointment. We can arrange factory visits, showroom tours, and guided market walks through Yiwu International Trade City.
